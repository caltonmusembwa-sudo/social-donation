01 / WHO WE ARE

A club with
a bigger purpose.

O&U Interact is a student-led service club at Onwards and Upwards Secondary School.

We bring students together to develop leadership skills, build friendships and take action on issues that matter. Through service projects and creative initiatives, we aim to make our school and community a better place.
